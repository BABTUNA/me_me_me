## The pitch

Most small businesses cannot afford the $5K to $50K agencies charge for storyboards and strategy. Without that budget, they skip real research, guess at messaging, and hope their ads work.

AdVantage flips that. You describe your business in plain language. The system runs **location-based research** (competitors, reviews, trends, SEO, and what local customers search for), then delivers three outputs in one flow:

1. A **research PDF** with market gaps, seasonal posting patterns, and keyword intel  
2. A **concept package** with storyboard, script, budget, filming locations, and hiring guide  
3. A **ready-to-post viral clip** for TikTok and Reels  

Everything is delivered through **ASI:One** and saved to **Google Drive**.

## What we built

AdVantage is a modular multi-agent pipeline:

- **Marky (research):** SerpAPI for competitors and related questions, Google and Yelp for reviews, Google Trends / DataForSEO for seasonality, plus a competitor map via Google Static Maps and Geocoding  
- **Intent and script:** Gemini parses natural-language input; a trend analyzer and script writer turn research into an ad script  
- **Storyboard:** Vertex AI Imagen generates pencil-sketch frames (with configurable delays to respect rate limits)  
- **Video:** FFmpeg for Ken Burns storyboard video; VEO 3, Lyria, and Google TTS for photorealistic viral output  
- **PDF and delivery:** ReportLab for the campaign PDF; a Google Drive MCP agent uploads assets; the orchestrator sends thumbnails and links in chat  

**Stack:** Python, Fetch.ai Chat Protocol, Agentverse / ASI:One, SerpAPI, Vertex AI (Imagen), Gemini, ReportLab, FFmpeg, Google Maps APIs, Google Drive MCP.

Each stage (research, script, images, video, PDF, Drive) is swappable so we can upgrade pieces without rewriting the whole system.

## The 36 hours

We got paid **$500 big ones** to stay up **36 hours straight**. From shipping new ideas every couple hours to nearly crashing out at 4 a.m., it was one of the most rewarding builds I have done.

Some problems we actually hit (and fixed):

- **Imagen rate limits** causing image failures → added `IMAGEN_DELAY_SECONDS` (30s default) between calls  
- **Black video thumbnails** → sample multiple frames and pick the first non-black one (mean brightness above 15)  
- **VEO + Lyria + TTS ordering** → careful pipeline timing so music and voiceover land correctly  
- **Agentverse storage webhooks** → fallbacks when async storage calls lag  
- **Competitor map missing addresses** → extended Marky’s Competitor Insight model with an address field for geocoding  
- **Robotic UX** → rewrote copy in plain language, added progress check-ins, simplified final replies to thumbnail + links  

## What I took away

Market research and creative generation work best as **separate agent stages**: research first, then script and visuals. Fetch’s Chat Protocol made it straightforward to add image previews and thumbnail-plus-links in ASI:One. Progress callbacks matter for long-running pipelines. Graceful fallbacks (maps when geocoding fails, storage when Agentverse is slow, delays for Imagen) kept demos from dying on stage.
